# An Extraordinary Dynasty of Fantasy Gentlemen

**The Shadow Dynasty** league constitution — Sleeper | Dynasty | Best Ball | Superflex

## Amendments

### February 2026

- Flipped rule stating there are unlimited Injured Reserve roster spots, because Injured Reserve slots are not supported in Sleeper Best Ball Leagues. The rule now clearly states that **there are no designated Injured Reserve slots in this league**.
- **Added a rule about the trade deadline**, which was not mentioned in these league rules, but was agreed upon and implemented in the inaugural season. Trading is not allowed during a brief period between the end of Week 12 games, and the end of the fantasy playoffs.
- Clarified that the **rookie draft includes league veterans who are free agents**, as well as all incoming rookie players.
- Clarified that the **waiver wire is locked from the end of the fantasy playoffs until two weeks after the rookie draft** (when final post-draft roster cuts are complete).
- Clarified that when the waiver wire re-opens after the rookie draft, **offseason and preseason waivers are processed once a week on a Wednesday**, with all unclaimed Free Agents and newly dropped players remaining on the waiver wire for another week until waivers are processed again the next Wednesday. There is no first-come, first-served Free Agency during the offseason and preseason.

## 1. League Overview

**Platform:** Sleeper
**Format:** Dynasty, Best Ball, Superflex

## 2. Purpose and Values

This league is meant to be **low-maintenance and easygoing**. The redraft league deserves our full attention when it comes to pedantic quibbling. This one's for people who want:

- A reason to be hyped about Isaac TeSlaa and Jacory Croskey-Merritt in during the offseason
- To be rewarded for long-term planning, youth-building, and stashing obscure sleepers
- To have a rookie draft to look forward to during the long, bleak offseason of discontent that comes around every year

## 3. Commissioners

To share the workload and keep things fair, the league will be overseen by four commissioners:

**Executive Commissioner**
- Resolves disputes and makes executive decisions on issues not covered by these league rules
- Has final say on rule interpretation and any grey areas

**Legislative Commissioner**
- Oversees the rule change process
- Manages votes on league rules and updates the constitution accordingly

**Draft Commissioner**
- Schedules and manages the rookie and startup drafts
- Ensures draft order is correct and all picks are recorded accurately
- Responsible for entering the draft outcomes into the app

**Financial Commissioner**
- Collects league fees each year
- Collects league fees for future years when future draft picks are traded
- Distributes payouts at the end of the season

## 4. Best Ball Format

- This is a Best Ball league
- Sleeper will automatically start your highest scoring players each week
- No need to set lineups each week
- Focus is entirely on **trades, drafting, and roster building**

## 5. League Settings

**Number of teams**
There will be 12 teams in the league. There are no divisions within the league.

**Scoring Format**
The scoring format is standard half-PPR. This is to keep things as simple as possible by using the same scoring format that is familiar to us from the redraft league.

**Starting Lineup Requirements**
This will be a *Superflex* league, which makes top-end QBs incredibly valuable, and even gives considerable value to consistent pocket-passers. This makes QBs much more significant in rookie drafts, which is much more interesting than just drafting RB/WR in the first round of every rookie draft.

Each team starts a whopping 10 players each week, meaning that 120 players are in starting lineups each week. Although this is a very large starting lineup for each team, the total number of players in starting lineups each week ends up being roughly the same as the redraft league (where there are 112 weekly starters). So, roughly the same pool of players will be relevant for starting lineups.

The lineup requirements will be as follows:
- 1 QB
- 1 SUPERFLEX (QB/WR/RB/TE)
- 2 RB
- 2 WR
- 1 TE
- 3 FLEX (WR/RB/TE)
- *No defense, no kicker*

## 6. Roster Management

**Total Roster Size**
Each team rosters **33 players**, meaning that altogether 396 players are rostered across the league. It will be an enormous task to draft all of these players initially. The startup draft is going to be incredibly long and exhausting, and you might not even recognise the players you are drafting by the latter rounds. You could be picking up rookies who might never actually get on the field.

However, enormous rosters have the significant advantage of leaving barely anyone as a free agent. This means that you shouldn't need to monitor the weekly waiver wire particularly closely during the season, because all of the players who become relevant after an injury, spike week, etc. should already be rostered.

The aim is for the in-season stress of roster management to therefore be largely removed from the league. After the initial effort of the startup draft, managing a team in this league could be relatively low maintenance for fairly large chunks of time.

However, when constructing your roster, you will need to put thought into planning whether you are competing or tanking in the short term, building depth at different positions, being patient with younger players, and trading intelligently to fill needs.

**Injured Reserve (IR)**
- There are no designated injured reserve spots. If your players are injured you must either hold them on your 33 player roster, or cut them from your roster, at which point they are available to be picked up by other teams just like any other player

**Taxi Squad**
- No taxi squad
- We play Best Ball. Stack your rookies on your huge roster. No roster juggling. Just draft well, build smart, and enjoy.

**Waiver Wire**
- The aim of the league is to make the waiver wire largely irrelevant
- Waiver order will be initiated as the reverse of the draft order
- Team moves to the end of the waiver order whenever they make a waiver pick
- The waiver wire is locked from the end of the playoffs until two weeks after the rookie draft (when final post-draft roster cuts are complete).
- When the waiver wire re-opens after the rookie draft, offseason and preseason waivers are processed once a week on a Wednesday, with all unclaimed Free Agents and newly dropped players remaining on the waiver wire for another week until waivers are processed again the next Wednesday. There are no first-come, first-served free agent pick-ups during the offseason and preseason.

## 7. Drafts

**Startup Draft**
- Will be a snake draft for ease and familiarity (avoids running the risk of players who are unfamiliar with auctions screwing the auction up, crippling their team from the outset, and losing interest quickly)
- There will be a third round reversal because the early QBs are so valuable in Superflex that this reversal helps to even out the playing field
- Draft order to be determined in a random manner, the specific mechanism of randomness to be decided upon by the Draft Commissioner
- All players (rookies and vets) included
- Trades of picks and players are allowed (see Trading section for details)
- Possibility of splitting the 33 round draft into two separate events to avoid fatigue

**Rookie Draft**
- Linear format, worst-to-first based on last season standings (including playoffs)
- Takes place annually some time in the month after the NFL Draft
- Scheduled and organised by the Draft Commissioner
- Includes all incoming rookie players, as well as any league veterans who are free agents
- 5 rounds
- After the draft, teams have two weeks to make trades and cut their roster size back down to the required 33 players.
- Two weeks after the draft, if any team has a roster size exceeding 33 players, the Draft Commissioner will auto-drop the least owned player by rostered % until there are only 33 players remaining.
- Picks can be traded (see Trading section)

## 8. League Standings and Playoffs

**Regular Season**
- Every team plays every other team at least once
- Any extra games are scheduled based on prior season standings — strong teams face similarly strong teams, weaker teams vs similarly weak teams, etc.
- League standings are determined based on win-loss record. In situations where two teams have the same wins and losses, total points scored is the tiebreaker. If total points scored is also tied, a coin flip will be the final tiebreaker.

**Playoffs**
- The top 4 teams in league standings qualify for the playoffs.
- There is no consolation bracket. If you didn't make the playoffs, your season is over and you should probably start scouting next year's rookie class.
- The first seed chooses which team they want to face in their semi final

## 9. Fees and Payouts

**Fees**
- $50 per season, paid annually before the Startup Draft / Rookie Draft

**Payouts**
- 1st Place – $300
- 2nd Place – $150
- 3rd Place – $100
- 4th Place – $50

## 10. League Governance

**Rule Changes**
The aim is to keep things simple and manageable. Rule changes can only be proposed and voted on **between the Super Bowl and the NFL Draft**.

- Managed by the Legislative Commissioner
- Any league member can propose a change by:
  1. Writing up the rule clearly (matching this constitution's tone/structure) and submitting it to the Legislative Commissioner for proof-reading
  2. Getting signatures from **3 other teams in the league** to support the proposal
     - Any amendments during this process of canvassing support for the proposal must also be proof-read by the Legislative Commissioner
  3. Once 3 other teams have also officially supported the proposal, a yes/no poll is posted for the entire league to vote on.
  4. If the majority (7 or more teams) approve the rule change, the rule is added to the constitution

See the **[Rule Change Log](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BDF2tGhG60PDYU5Uh04yzENIliTJ7c501UCbt5uQr7U/edit?gid=0#gid=0)** for the history of proposed, voted, and adopted changes.

**Dispute Resolution and Executive Decisions**
If there's a dispute or anything not covered by this constitution, the **Executive Commissioner makes the final call**.

## 11. Trading

- Trading is allowed year-round, except during a brief period between the end of Week 12 games, and the end of the fantasy playoffs.
- Trades that involve "borrowing and returning" players are not allowed
- You may trade draft picks from the upcoming draft, as well as a subsequent additional 2 drafts in the future
- Before trading away any future picks, you must pay league fees for that future year immediately
- This rule ensures that no manager can wreck a team and walk away without consequences, and that incoming managers or orphaned teams don't have to pay league fees while they dig themselves out of holes.

## 12. Orphan Teams and League Longevity

**League Longevity**
The whole point of the dynasty format is that the league continues indefinitely, and long term planning is required. It is unfair to teams who have potentially suffered short term pain in order to build assets for future years, if the league is suddenly abandoned as their strategy begins to bear fruit.

The aim is for this dynasty league to go on forever — with franchises passed down to our children, and our children's children as a prized asset. This should be an easygoing, low maintenance, fun way to stay in touch and have something to look forward to even in the NFL's dark offseason months.

**Orphaned Teams**
Circumstances change, and it is understandable if after a number of years, someone wants to bail on the league during the offseason. In this case, it is highly appreciated when they can work with the league to find a replacement to take over their team. The Executive Commissioner has final say on who joins the league.

If an orphaned team is missing future pick assets, it's not ideal — but that's why advance payment is required when trading them away. New managers will not have to pay league fees for those first few years where they are missing draft picks.

**Disbanding the League**
The league cannot be disbanded during the season.

If **8 or more teams** agree to disband the league in the offseason:
- The league will be disbanded
- Every manager who voted to keep the league alive must receive one year's league fees as compensation, with the payment split evenly amongst those leaving.

If **3–7 teams** try to abandon the league all at the same time in the offseason, without replacements immediately available:
- That's a real dick move, because it will probably cripple the league
- Regardless of whether the league survives, those leaving must ensure that each remaining manager receives one year's league fees as compensation, with the payment split evenly amongst those leaving
